# Status

[Version 1.0.0](https://github.com/DannyExperiments/majority-3cnf-one-long-clause/releases/tag/v1.0.0) · 27 November 2026 · [Version DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24569554)

Testing whether a 3-CNF conjoined with one unrestricted clause satisfies at least half of the uniform assignments is PP-complete under polynomial-time many-one reductions. The proof also establishes PP-completeness for comparing a 2-CNF's satisfaction probability to an input power-of-two threshold. These are Akmal's Open Problems 8 and 9.

Internal mathematical review covered the statement, proof and prior ingredients. External peer review and formal verification have not been conducted. The exact finite replay covers the worked examples at, below and above the threshold, and checks the three reduction components on small instances. See the [mathematics summary](audits/public_safe_reports/MATHEMATICS.md) and [literature review](audits/public_safe_reports/LITERATURE.md).
